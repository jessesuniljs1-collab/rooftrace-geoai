Panel & roof assumptions

These are editable scenario assumptions. Imagery does not measure obstructions, structural strength, actual roof pitch or local shading. Panel fit is a 2D flat-roof illustration; module tilt affects energy only. PVGIS includes regional terrain-horizon effects.

Building AI identifies candidate regions. A transparent geometry model fits panel scenarios. PVGIS estimates regional energy yield. Every assumption remains visible.

What is measured and modeled

Roof regions come from a real UNet++ / EfficientNet-B4 model. Scale comes from sample metadata or your calibration. PVGIS ERA5 supplies regional climate-based yield; local shade and structural suitability require a site assessment.

Google Satellite context

The free Google Satellite link opens the selected location for visual reference. No Google tiles are downloaded or analyzed, and no Google API key or billing account is used. Upload only imagery you have rights to analyze.
