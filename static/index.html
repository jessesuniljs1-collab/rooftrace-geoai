BUILT FOR A FIRST PASS

Visible results. Clear boundaries.

RoofTrace helps you review visible building regions. It is an experimental mapping aid, with no claim of survey accuracy.

What the model sees

A building-specific UNet++ / EfficientNet-B4 model trained on WHU RGB aerial images at 0.3 m per pixel. Fresh CPU inference runs on every analysis, with 512 px overlapping tiles for larger images.

Where it can miss

Resolution, viewing angle, trees, shadows and adjoining roofs affect results. Low-resolution imagery may produce substantial errors. PNG/JPG files carry no reliable geographic scale, so exported coordinates are pixels, not map locations.

Sources & attribution

Three WHU test images are provided for research demonstration under the hosted release’s CC BY 4.0 terms. The Phoenix sample uses public-domain USDA/USGS imagery and demonstrates transfer limitations.
